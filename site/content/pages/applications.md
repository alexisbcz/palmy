# Applications on Palmframe

Every palmy application builds for Palmframe. Whether it is useful there depends on what it needs at runtime, and Palmframe ships very little beyond the base system: no ffmpeg, PipeWire, NetworkManager, BlueZ, OpenSSL or ollama.

Of the {{appTotal}} entries below, **{{appYes}} work** on the bare system, **{{appPartial}} work partly** and **{{appNo}} do not work yet**. {{appDefault}} are installed by default; the rest are not packaged, or are in the repository for `palm add` to fetch.
