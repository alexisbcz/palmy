# The window manager, carrying the film.
# 
# The window manager's status bar names the spawn *slot*, not the binary that
# film/tools/capture/sandbox.sh bound to it: a permanent legend reading
# "F1 counter  F2 gallery ...", and a toast saying "launched counter" for
# three seconds after each launch (`wall_now() - s.status_at < 3.0`). Both sit
# in the right-hand end of the bar, and every window-manager shot in
# shots.json carries a `mask` over exactly that region -- verified by reading
# the bar back out of the finished cut. No frame of the film shows either.
#
# The marked line below is where the last toast has expired anyway, and the
# shot list takes its desktop footage from there on. The mask is what makes
# the wallpaper shot at the head of the clip safe as well.
#
