# Driven against the left window (Alice), which dials Bob on 127.0.0.1:47812.
# Bob is the right window and answers by itself, so the call is up before the
# recording starts and both windows show the other one's picture.
#
# Both instances offer H.264 and MJPEG, so the call runs on H.264 at about
# 300 kbit/s; the Statistics panel names the codec and both offers that chose
# it, and the keyframe and loss-recovery rows below it are the H.264 story.
#
# Synthetic sources only: a 440 Hz tone modulated at 0.4 Hz, ffmpeg's testsrc2
# on the left and SMPTE bars on the right. Nothing here opens the camera or
# the microphone; see record.sh and resolve_capture() in main.odin.
#
# Everything in this file becomes one wtype argv — see the note at the top of
# record.sh for why — so `wait` is wtype's own `-s` and the pacing is exact.

wait 2.5

# The live numbers first: the negotiated codec, measured latency, the jitter
# buffer, measured frame rate in and out, keyframes, and the packet counters
# both ways.
wait 4.5

# Mute: the left meter falls to silence, and the badge appears on the right
# window's tile for Alice, because mute travels in the STATE control message.
key m
wait 3.0
key m
wait 2.5

# Camera off: the right window shows the camera-off placeholder where Alice's
# video was, and the left window's self-view says so too. The self-view is its
# own H.264 decode of our own stream, scaled to the size of the tile.
key v
wait 3.0
key v
wait 3.0

# The device picker: the real microphone and camera, enumerated from wpctl and
# v4l2-ctl, listed and refused.
key d
wait 3.0

wait 1.0

wait 1.0
key Right
wait 2.0

key c
