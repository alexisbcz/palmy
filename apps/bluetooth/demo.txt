# Bluetooth applet demo.
#
# Everything here is read-only or additive: a bounded scan, keyboard
# navigation, and the action menu opened but cancelled. Nothing is unpaired,
# untrusted or disconnected.

wait 1.6
# The adapter panel, and the paired headset under the pointer.
move 330 200
wait 2.0

# A real, bounded scan: bluetoothctl --timeout 9 scan on, on a worker thread.
key s
wait 2.0
move 330 330
wait 9.0
wait 3.0

# Keyboard navigation through what the scan found.
key Down
